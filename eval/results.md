## Full answers

**Q1. How many days of annual leave do I get after my probation ends?**

- Expected: 20 working days per calendar year (25 after more than 5 years of continuous service).
- Answer: After your probation ends, you will receive 20 working days of paid annual leave per calendar year. If you have more than 5 years of continuous service, this increases to 25 working days. Part-time employees will receive leave based on their contracted hours. Public holidays are in addition to this annual leave.
- Citations: HR-01 | 3. Entitlement
- Result: correct answer

**Q2. How many unused leave days can I carry over into next year, and when do I have to use them?**

- Expected: Up to 5 days; carried-over days must be used by 31 March or they are lost.
- Answer: According to the Annual Leave Policy, you can carry over up to 5 unused leave days into the next calendar year. However, you must use these carried-over days by 31 March of the following year, or they will be lost. Any unused days beyond the 5-day limit will also be lost at the end of the year unless you get written approval from the Head of People for an exception.
- Citations: HR-01 | 5. Carrying over leave
- Result: correct answer

**Q3. When do I need a medical certificate for sick leave?**

- Expected: For any absence of more than 2 consecutive working days.
- Answer: You need a medical certificate if you are absent for more than 2 consecutive working days. This requirement is outlined in the Sick Leave Policy under the section about medical certificates.
- Citations: HR-02 | 3. Medical certificate
- Result: correct answer

**Q4. Which days am I expected to be in the office?**

- Expected: Tuesday and Thursday are anchor days for hybrid staff (at least 2 office days per week).
- Answer: According to the Hybrid Working Model policy, you are expected to be in the Lagos office at least 2 days per week, specifically on Tuesday and Thursday, which are designated as anchor days for all hybrid staff. On other days, you can work from home if you have your line manager's agreement.
- Citations: HR-05 | 1. Hybrid working model
- Result: correct answer

**Q5. How far in advance do I need to request an international trip?**

- Expected: At least 21 days before departure.
- Answer: According to the Business Travel Policy, you need to request an international trip at least 21 days before your departure. If you submit your request late, it may be refused or require approval from a Director.
- Citations: FIN-03 | 2. Booking
- Result: correct answer

**Q6. What is the deadline for submitting an expense claim?**

- Expected: Within 30 days of the expense, in the Spendly expense tool.
- Answer: You need to submit your expense claims in the Spendly expense tool within 30 days of incurring the expense. This is outlined in the Expense Reimbursement Policy.
- Citations: FIN-01 | 2. Submitting claims
- Result: correct answer

**Q7. I lost my work laptop. What should I do?**

- Expected: Report it to IT Support within 24 hours; IT will remotely lock and wipe it. If stolen, also report to the police and share the reference with IT.
- Answer: If you've lost your work laptop, you need to report it to IT Support at it-support@kestrelnova.example within 24 hours. They will take action to remotely lock and wipe the device. Additionally, if it was stolen, you must report the theft to the police and share the report reference with IT.
- Citations: IT-01 | 2. Loss or theft
- Result: correct answer

**Q8. What is my yearly budget for training and courses?**

- Expected: NGN 400,000 per calendar year; unused budget does not carry over.
- Answer: According to the Learning and Development Policy, your yearly budget for training and courses is NGN 400,000. This amount is available for courses, certifications, conferences, and books, but keep in mind that any unused budget does not carry over to the next year.
- Citations: HR-07 | 1. Annual budget
- Result: correct answer

**Q9. How much paternity leave do I get?**

- Expected: 10 working days at full pay, taken within 3 months of the birth or adoption, in up to two blocks.
- Answer: According to the Parental Leave Policy, you are entitled to 10 working days of paternity leave at full pay. This leave must be taken within 3 months of your child's birth or adoption and can be taken in up to two separate blocks.
- Citations: HR-04 | 2. Paternity leave
- Result: correct answer

**Q10. A supplier sent me a gift worth NGN 25,000. Can I keep it?**

- Expected: Not automatically. Gifts above NGN 15,000 must be declared to Compliance within 5 working days; Compliance decides whether it is kept, returned or donated.
- Answer: According to the Code of Conduct on Gifts, you cannot keep the gift worth NGN 25,000 without declaring it first. Since it exceeds the limit of NGN 15,000, you need to inform Compliance within 5 working days. They will then decide if you can keep it, return it, or donate it.
- Citations: CMP-02 | 2. Gifts
- Result: correct answer

**Q11. I stayed in a hotel costing NGN 70,000 per night on a domestic work trip. Is that within the limit, and who needs to approve my claim?**

- Expected: Yes, the domestic limit is NGN 80,000 per night. Claim items above NGN 50,000 need line manager and Finance approval.
- Answer: Your hotel cost of NGN 70,000 per night is within the limit for domestic travel, which is NGN 80,000 per night. Since your expense is above NGN 50,000, you will need approval from both your line manager and Finance for your claim.
- Citations: FIN-03 | 4. Accommodation, FIN-01 | 3. Approvals
- Result: correct answer

**Q12. The company paid NGN 300,000 for my certification. If I resign 8 months after finishing it, what do I owe, and what happens to my unused annual leave?**

- Expected: You repay 50% of the cost (NGN 150,000), deducted from final salary where possible. Unused annual leave for the current year is paid out at your basic daily rate in the final salary.
- Answer: Since the company paid NGN 300,000 for your certification and you are resigning 8 months after completing it, you will owe 50% of the cost, which is NGN 150,000. This amount will be deducted from your final salary. Regarding your unused annual leave, any accrued but unused leave will be paid out at your basic daily rate in your final salary. If you took more leave than you accrued, that will be deducted from your final salary as well.
- Citations: HR-07 | 4. Repayment, HR-01 | 6. Leaving the company
- Result: correct answer
